Why Online

Reading online, without the theatre.

An online reading is a real conversation with a real person, carried over chat, phone, or video instead of across a table. Nothing about the format changes what happens once you're in the session: you bring what's on your mind, your reader responds, and you decide how long to stay. If it isn't working for you, you can end the session any time. Nothing holds you in the chair, and there's no travel to plan around.

Every conversation runs over an encrypted connection, so what you say stays between you and your reader. You don't need to clear an evening or leave the house. A session fits into a lunch break or a quiet hour at home just as well as a Saturday afternoon. And once it's over, nothing disappears: your transcripts and notes stay in your account, so you can reread a line that mattered later, without having to trust your own memory of exactly how it was worded.

Before You Book

What to bring to your session.

The questions that get the most out of a reading tend to be open ones. Something like "what should I watch for in this relationship" gives a reader more to work with than "will he call me back," and you'll usually walk away with more than a flat yes or no.

It also helps to pick one topic and stay with it, rather than jumping from your career to your family to a decision you made last week. Bring the context that actually matters, names, dates, what's already happened, instead of holding facts back to test your reader. A reading works better as a conversation than as a quiz, and a reader can go deeper on one thread than on five shallow ones.

Works well

  • What should I focus on in my career right now?
  • What patterns keep showing up in my relationships?
  • What does this next chapter look like for me?
  • How can I approach this decision with more clarity?
  • What do I need to let go of to move forward?

Better asked elsewhere

  • Medical diagnoses or symptoms
  • Legal decisions and contracts
  • Investment or financial moves
  • Anything that needs a licensed professional

Readings are meant for guidance and reflection. They aren't a substitute for advice from a doctor, lawyer, or financial professional, and no reader can promise a specific outcome. If you're not sure whether a question fits, our FAQ covers it, or you can book a reading and ask your reader directly how they'd frame it.

Questions We Get Asked

Questions we get asked.

How long should a first reading be?

Most first-time clients do fine with a shorter session, enough time to ask your main question and follow it where it goes, without committing to a full hour before you know how the connection feels. You can extend a chat or phone session on the spot if it's going well, or book something longer once you've found a reader you trust. Our pricing page breaks down cost by length.

What happens if the connection doesn't feel right?

End it. Every format lets you close a session whenever you want, and trying a different reader afterward costs you nothing but the time. Not every pairing clicks, and that's normal. A reading depends on fit as much as skill. If you're unsure who to try next, reach out through contact and we'll point you toward someone else.

Do I need to prepare anything before a session?

Not really. No forms, no printed birth chart, no memorized script, just have your question in mind and enough quiet that you can talk or type without getting interrupted. If you're booking a video session, decent lighting and a plain background help more than any list of prep. Our video page covers the basics.

How does privacy and session records work?

Every session runs over an encrypted connection, and only you can see your own reading history. Chat transcripts and notes from phone or video sessions stay attached to your account so you can revisit them later; they're never visible to other readers or shared outside the platform. Our FAQ covers the details if you want more.

How does pricing work?

Each format bills by the minute at a rate shown before you connect, so there's no flat fee to commit to upfront. Watch the time as the session runs and end it whenever you've gotten what you needed. If you'd rather talk it through with a real person first, call through our phone line before you book.
